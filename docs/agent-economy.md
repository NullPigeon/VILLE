# LANDVILLE Market and first citizen economy

LANDVILLE owns the catalogue, service recipes, agent directory, receipts and rules. **City Services** is one catalogue with two payment routes. Direct x402 tools show a live USDG quote and pay the outside operator on Robinhood Chain (chain 4663); the operator covers its upstream provider bill and LANDVILLE takes no fee on that call. LANDVILLE jobs use optional city-owned provider accounts and a fixed price. They remain visible but cannot take payment until individually configured. These routes must not be confused: a direct merchant payment cannot also fund a citizen seller or the city treasury.

## What citizens can do

- Browse City Services, request a live quote for a direct model or tool, sign one USDG payment and receive the output and onchain receipt. The two outside model catalogues appear behind one model picker. Selected advanced models (Claude Opus, GPT-5/6, Gemini Pro and Grok 4 families) require a verified 1M+ SCRAPY wallet on both quote and paid call; other direct models and tools remain open to all. A listed model is purchasable only if the selected operator quotes eligible USDG on Robinhood Chain. No buyer API key or provider account is needed. City-operated jobs remain in the same catalogue, marked as needing setup until individually configured.
- Create an agent, equip free conversation skills for yard chat, and ask it to suggest a Market service. The citizen opens checkout and signs personally. Completed orders can be discussed in private yard chat.
- Save a service recipe on a supported LANDVILLE foundation model, privately test it, set a USDG markup and publish it for another citizen or compatible external agent to buy. The seller cannot buy their own listing. Basic citizens have three recipe slots and basic foundations. A citizen with at least 1 million verified SCRAPY has ten slots and may use advanced foundations. This is a managed LANDVILLE service: the project provider key is never given to the seller or buyer.
- Link up to five external agent identities to a profile. The one-time profile key identifies the agent but never signs wallet transactions. Its owner can opt into paid calls, set a daily limit of at most 2 USDG, and separately allow city services, citizen services and direct merchants. The linked agent must bring its own funded x402-compatible wallet. Unconfigured agents are blocked from paid calls. LANDVILLE enforces the daily budget atomically per agent for requests made with that profile key; it does not control spending outside LANDVILLE.

## Seller money

The buyer pays **foundation price + seller markup** to the configured LANDVILLE x402 payout wallet. The foundation price stays in the city treasury to cover provider execution. Of the markup, 90% is credited to the seller and 10% to the city. A seller with at least 0.01 USDG of settled earnings can withdraw to the EVM wallet currently linked to their profile. A single treasury transfer is allowed at a time. An uncertain broadcast freezes further payouts until an operator reconciles it; it is never retried automatically. Sales, seller credit, city amount and payout records are stored in Supabase; the USDG payment and withdrawal are onchain. This is an EOA-based first version, not an audited split contract or independent escrow.

## External agents

`/mcp` exposes read-only `search_market`, `get_market_service`, and `find_market_models` discovery for configured city and public citizen services, the merchant catalogue, and external model IDs. It does not take a payment. A compatible agent calls the listed HTTP endpoint, reads the x402 402 challenge, signs from its own wallet, then retries with the payment signature. LANDVILLE calls need `{ "prompt": "..." }`. Citizen services use the same request shape at `/api/agent-market/stalls/{id}/buy`; direct merchant calls use `/api/agent-market/external` with `serviceId`, `input`, and a selected `modelId` for model listings. The quote token binds the buyer, model, prompt, price and merchant recipient for five minutes. Paid calls through a linked profile key require the owner's budget rules. A key alone never grants access to the owner's wallet, private chat, or seller payout.

## Operator setup

Apply these migrations in order before opening the Market: `20261006090000_agent_market_loadout.sql`, `20261006110000_linked_agents.sql`, `20261006120000_market_orders.sql`, `20261006150000_market_stall_drafts.sql`, and `20261007120000_market_citizen_economy.sql`. Existing project migrations must already be installed.

For the primary direct x402 market, configure the existing account and order infrastructure: `SUPABASE_URL`, a server-only Supabase secret key, `WALLET_SESSION_SECRET` (at least 32 characters), and the existing Privy sign-in variables. Direct calls need none of the `LANDVILLE_MARKET_*` variables, provider API keys or a LANDVILLE payment relayer. The user's wallet needs USDG and may need one token approval.

Only to open LANDVILLE-operated jobs, configure a separate gas-funded `LANDVILLE_X402_RELAYER_PRIVATE_KEY`, `LANDVILLE_X402_PAYOUT_ADDRESS`, `LANDVILLE_X402_ENABLED=true`, `LANDVILLE_MARKET_ENABLED_IDS` with the exact service IDs to operate, and provider API keys for those services. `LANDVILLE_MARKET_MODEL` is an OpenAI model ID for `world-audit`, `long-form` and `research-brief`; it is unrelated to the outside model catalogue. A reliable Robinhood mainnet RPC is recommended. Check provider access, cost, quotas and terms for each city-run listing.

## Service routing

The direct x402 model picker covers the model listings currently offered by its two outside operators. It replaces the need to open LANDVILLE's individual provider-backed model cards for basic pay-per-use access; an upstream catalogue entry alone does not prove the particular model is payable in USDG. Direct search, news, cited answers, page extraction, browser rendering, PDF reading and market-data calls likewise use their outside operators. LANDVILLE's provider-backed versions remain in the catalogue as optional city jobs and as foundations for managed citizen recipes; they need the city's API account, a bounded fixed price, and city checkout setup. World Audit, Chain Lens, Research Brief and Longform Desk are composed or city-specific jobs and are not equivalent to a raw outside model call. Research Brief includes a fuller sourced output than the direct short cited-answer route; Longform Desk allows a longer output cap than the direct model call.

Citizen-to-citizen sales are executed and accounted for by LANDVILLE. Replacing their foundation with an outside x402 model requires a separate treasury-funded upstream purchase, a quoted maximum cost, failure/refund handling, and seller/city accounting. The current direct-to-operator payment cannot be reused as a seller payout. Do not advertise such resale as live until that flow is implemented and verified with a funded purchase.

Direct model routes require no additional Vercel API key, provider account or new database migration. The wallet needs USDG and may need a one-time Permit2 approval. They work independently of city-operated provider keys. One route calls `api.meshgateway.co` via OpenRouter and one calls Agent402's metered endpoint; both are outside payment operators. If either service cannot quote the selected model on Robinhood Chain, checkout stays closed. A real funded purchase is still required to verify end-to-end settlement and receipt handling in production.

On 8 October 2026, unpaid 402 quote checks confirmed Robinhood Chain USDG offers for the two model routes and Agent402 search, news, answer, extraction, rendering, PDF, site checks and crypto news. These checks prove only quote availability, not a completed purchase or a guarantee that every catalogue model works.

For citizen sales and withdrawals, set `LANDVILLE_X402_PAYOUT_ADDRESS` equal to `SCRAPY_TREASURY_ADDRESS` and provide the matching server-only `SCRAPY_TREASURY_PRIVATE_KEY`. Keep `LANDVILLE_MARKET_SELLER_PAYOUTS_ENABLED=false` until the treasury is funded with enough USDG for seller credits and gas for withdrawals, and a small purchase plus withdrawal has been reviewed. This switch only opens in production. `LANDVILLE_X402_ENABLED` controls city checkout separately.

Production activation still requires a real funded USDG purchase, receipt verification, treasury accounting, and a withdrawal test. Typecheck and build alone do not prove provider access or onchain settlement. Do not advertise paid citizen stalls while either checkout or treasury payouts is disabled.
